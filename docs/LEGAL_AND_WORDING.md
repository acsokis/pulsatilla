# Legal and wording notes

Reviewed against the primary sources below on 2026-10-05. This is a source-based editorial
and licensing check, not a legal opinion, clearance of the Pulsatilla name, or approval of
future service contracts. A lawyer should review the actual sales flow and terms before launch.

## Product wording

Public product documentation describes Pulsatilla's own implemented capabilities and limits.
Competitor comparisons, competitor feature-page links, rankings, price/value claims and claims
of being a replacement or imitation have been removed. Necessary runtime/dependency names
are factual technical references, not sponsorship or endorsement. No competitor logos are used.

[German UWG § 6](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/uwg_2004/__6.html) defines comparative
advertising broadly, including indirect identification of a competitor. It regulates objectivity,
verifiability, confusion, unfair reputation use, denigration and imitation claims. Simply adding
"no opinion" or "no affiliation" would not establish that a comparison is lawful. The editorial
choice here is to omit competitor comparisons entirely.

The relevant EU framework is [Directive 2006/114/EC, Article 4](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32006L0114),
which sets conditions for comparative advertising. These sources do not establish trademark
availability for the new product name. No trademark clearance has been performed.

"Network clarity. Security insight." is a product slogan, not a documented benchmark or guarantee
of protection. Security findings remain review indicators. Imported reports do not prove
current exposure or a clean account. The app is not marketed as a live dark-web-monitoring
service, antivirus or VPN when those services are not implemented.

## MIT and earnings

The [MIT license](https://choosealicense.com/licenses/mit/) permits commercial use, modification
and redistribution subject to retaining the copyright/license notice. It is deliberately
permissive; it does not reserve exclusive monetization rights. Copyright is attributed to
Gábor Kocsis. Paid hosted services, support and bespoke work can be offered separately with
their own accurate descriptions and applicable terms.

The exact MIT text is preserved in `LICENSE` and embedded in the app. It includes warranty
and liability clauses. It does not create a universal exemption from responsibility.
For example, [German BGB § 309 no. 7](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__309.html)
restricts certain liability exclusions in standard terms involving injury, gross negligence
or intentional misconduct. Which rules apply depends on the actual transaction and parties.
No document in this repository says that the creator is never liable.

Seller information, consumer rights, taxes, privacy obligations and paid-service terms depend
on the actual service and market. This preparation does not claim those obligations are fulfilled.
